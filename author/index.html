Behind Every World Is a Curious Mind.

“I'm Thisha Smith, a storyteller who often finds inspiration in the unexpected—a name, a piece of art, a scene, or a situation that makes me wonder, what if?”

“I'm interested in people as they are, the decisions they make, and who they become when life challenges them. My imagination moves freely across genres and storytelling formats, from fictional worlds to interactive and cinematic experiences.”

“I believe every story can leave something different with each person. Sometimes it's a character. Sometimes it's a feeling, a question, or a moment they won't forget.”

“Life is full of choices. Every choice leaves a story.”

Looking back on life changes how we understand the choices we've made. Sometimes we imagine doing things differently. Sometimes we realize those decisions helped shape who we've become.

Through storytelling, I explore those possibilities—the choices characters make, the consequences they face, and the paths their lives might take.
