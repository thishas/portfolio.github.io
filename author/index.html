Beyond the page

A story can begin with anything.

A name. A piece of art. A place. A moment that makes me wonder, what if?

I'm Thisha Smith, an author and storyteller drawn to the lives people lead, the choices they face, and the possibilities that follow. My imagination doesn't stay in one genre or one kind of world. I enjoy discovering who characters are today—and watching who they become when life asks something difficult of them.

I'm continuing to grow as a creator, exploring original fiction, interactive stories, and cinematic experiences. I don't expect every reader to take away the same thing. A character, a feeling, a question, or a memorable moment may stay with each person differently.

Creative philosophy

Different stories. Different worlds. Something to remember.

I'm interested in who people appear to be, who they become, and the distance between the two. Growing older changes how we look at the decisions we've made; storytelling lets me explore what might happen if a choice went another way.

Not every story needs to offer a lesson, and not every ending needs to be perfect. Sometimes an adventure is simply an adventure. Sometimes a decision changes everything.
